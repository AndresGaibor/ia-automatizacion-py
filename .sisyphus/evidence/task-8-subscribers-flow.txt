ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY

New POM structure for subscribers (following tasks 5-7 patterns):

  - SubscribersPage(BasePage)
  - navigate_to_detail(campaign_id)  → /report/campaign/{id}/ + click "Detalles suscriptores"

  - select_filter(label) → #query-filter select_option

  - extract_table_data() → returns SubscriberTableData(correo, lista, estado, calidad)

  - SubscribersFlow

  - extract_hard_bounces_and_no_abiertos(campaign, campaign_id, config) → CampaignSubscriberReport

utils/selectors.py:
